## Brand & Style

The brand personality projects academic prestige, institutional authority, ambitious upward mobility, and professional vitality. Built specifically for graduates, enrolled scholars, alumni, and industry recruiters, the experience bridges rigorous higher education with modern corporate placement. The interface must inspire trust and pride, presenting students not merely as academic profiles, but as industry-ready talent.

The visual style is **Corporate / Modern** anchored by precise editorial tension and authoritative badge systems. It avoids childish campus metaphors in favor of sleek, executive-level presentation reminiscent of high-end fintech and modern executive networks. Sharp typographic weights, crisp edge contrasts, and deliberate institutional heraldic accents give the app a verified, high-trust atmosphere.

## Colors

The core palette balances Richfield's signature vibrant Crimson Red (`#C4122F`) for high-intent primary calls to action, notifications, and key milestones with an authoritative Deep Royal Navy Blue (`#0F2C59`) for navigation shells, top headers, and verified student structures. A tertiary Sovereign Gold (`#D4AF37`) is deployed exclusively for verification highlights, academic honors, and recruiter endorsements.

## Typography

Inter serves across all typography hierarchies to deliver neutral legibility, sharp numerals, and an institutional yet high-tech mobile presence. Micro-labels, credential indicators, and verification tags utilize tightened, uppercase styling (`label-badge`) with deliberate letter spacing for immediate scanning.

Headlines employ tight negative tracking to impart an authoritative, editorial density, while body copy preserves generous line heights for clear reading of project summaries, theses, and recruiter correspondence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes an 8pt dynamic fluid grid system customized for mobile screens (375px to 428px standard viewports) extending to compact tablet formats.

- **Mobile Viewports (< 600px):** Single-column vertical stream with `16px` outer screen margins and `12px` card-nesting gutters.
- **Tablet / Large Handhelds (600px - 840px):** 2-column masonry or balanced multi-column grid with `24px` horizontal padding and `16px` gutters.
- **Form factor rhythm:** Interactive tap targets maintain minimum hit areas of `44x44px`. All card insets enforce `16px` internal padding, expanding to `20px` on featured showcase portfolios.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **low-contrast outlines** paired with subtle **ambient shadows**, preserving a structured, professional texture without unnecessary heavy skeuomorphism.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** No elevation; flat background tone.
- **Level 1 (Standard Feed Cards, List Elements):** `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 44, 89, 0.05)`, bordered with a `1px` crisp outline (`#E2E8F0` in light mode, `#1E293B` in dark mode).
- **Level 2 (Active States, Portfolios, Verification Drawers):** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 44, 89, 0.08)`, matching the royal blue tonal shadow depth.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Bottom Sheets, Profile Popovers):** `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.
- **Badge Layers:** Verification badges use a fine inner-stroke ring (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`) that confers a medal-like, certified finish.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft** shape language (`roundedness: 1`). Baseline elements adopt `4px` (`0.25rem`) to `8px` (`0.5rem`) radiuses to retain an institutional, structured, and certified quality. Rounded corners never approach playful bubbles; pills are used exclusively for verification chips, skill badges, and status pills.

## Components

### Verification Badges
- **Student Verification Badge:** Features a dual-tone configuration. Royal Blue base (`#0F2C59`) with gold metallic insignia (`#D4AF37`), displaying an academic seal icon and label: `@my.richfield.ac.za Verified`. A subtle micro-pill styling with `4px` padding and crisp hairline border.
- **Recruiter Verified Badge:** Emerald green badge (`#059669` / light tint background `#ECFDF5`) with checkmark shield verifying accredited company status.

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Direct Placement / Apply / Endorse):** Richfield Crimson (`#C4122F`), solid fill, white text (`label-lg`), `8px` corner radius, subtle bottom edge emphasis.
- **Secondary Action (View Portfolio / Connect):** Royal Navy (`#0F2C59`), solid fill or high-contrast crisp outline (`1.5px` border with `#0F2C59` text).
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Zero container fill, high-contrast medium-emphasis slate text with instant state transitions.

### Portfolio Cards
- **Structure:** Multi-layered card featuring student profile metadata header, primary degree discipline (`BSc IT`, `BCom Business Admin`), verified institution tag, preview media carousel or project thumbnail, key skill pills, and an action footer (Quick Endorse / Download Academic Transcript / Schedule Interview).
- **Surface:** Level 1 elevation with a subtle left accent stripe (Crimson for top achievers/summa cum laude, Royal Blue for standard alumni).

### Input Fields & Search
- Surface utilizes Slate 100 (`#F1F5F9`) background in light mode with a `1px` perimeter border (`#E2E8F0`). On focus, transitions cleanly to a `2px` border in Royal Blue (`#0F2C59`) with no harsh glowing rings.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Height: `32px`. Rounded pill shape (`rounded-full`). Unselected state: Slate border with Slate 600 text. Selected state: Deep Royal Navy fill with white text, or subtle Crimson fill for urgency/recruiting alerts.
